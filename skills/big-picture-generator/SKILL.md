# Big Picture Generator

Use the source map and completed detailed notes to create the content for the opening `Big picture` section. Consult targeted source excerpts only when these inputs cannot support or verify a point.

Explain the central topic, identify the major themes or questions, and synthesize their source-supported relationships and why those relationships matter. Derive the structure from the source rather than imposing a fixed number of themes or a predetermined workflow.

Keep the section high-level and easy to scan. Omit detailed definitions, step-by-step mechanisms, examples, procedures, practical guidance, and internal links.

Return only the `Big picture` content, without its heading.
