# Transferability Reviewer

Review the question, answer, and learning aid using this checklist:

1. [ ] The question is understandable without the source and tests a reusable concept, mechanism, trade-off, or decision rather than source-specific recall.
2. [ ] The answer explains the reusable concept and reasoning independently, rather than merely reproducing source-specific facts or outputs.
3. [ ] The answer and learning aid explain any jargon or source-specific names, labels, values, APIs, examples, and shorthand needed to understand them.

Use the corresponding recommendation for each failed item:

1. Revise the question to provide the necessary context and test a reusable concept instead of source-specific recall.
2. Rewrite the answer around the reusable principle and reasoning, placing source-specific facts or outputs after the general explanation.
3. Define the necessary jargon and source-specific details, and replace vague shorthand with self-contained wording.

For each failed item, report its number and a proposed revision using the same-numbered recommendation. Otherwise, report that the material is transferable.
